## Full description

```
Did you hit today's target, and is your streak still alive?

Padhai Streak is a simple study timer for Indian exam aspirants preparing for JEE, NEET, UPSC, SSC, CAT and board exams. Start the clock for a subject, and see where your hours really went. It works in English and हिंदी, with no login and no internet needed.

WHY STUDENTS USE IT

• A streak that means something
A day only counts when you cross your own daily target. Today never breaks your streak while it is still in progress, only a day you really missed does.

• Focus rounds
Pick Starter 15/5, Classic 25/5, Deep 50/10, your own length, or an open timer. The phone can stay face-down: an alarm tells you when the round is over.

• Honest about your phone
You can leave the app during a round, but it counts how many times you did, and your stats show how many rounds were clean.

• Subject-wise tracking
See hours for each subject this week or all time, and get a nudge when one subject has gone a week untouched.

• Exam countdown
Your exam and the days left, right on the home screen.

• Stats you can read
7-day and 30-day charts against your target, a 12-week calendar, your best streak and total hours.

• Made for late nights
Light and dark themes, and a daily reminder at the time you choose.

• Full Hindi support
The whole app works in Hindi, not only the menus, down to hours and minutes.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

No account. No ads. No in-app purchases. Your study history is stored on your phone and we never receive it. Save a backup file whenever you want and restore it on a new phone.

Privacy policy: https://stockbook.sellertoolsindia.com/padhai-streak/privacy.html

Padhai Streak is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with NTA, UPSC, SSC, any exam body or any government organisation. Exam names are used only to describe who the app is for.
```
